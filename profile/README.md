## Project Information
The rise of edge computing, combined with advanced 5G connectivity, has enabled large-scale deployments of private edge systems, driving innovation across various industries and advancing market digitization.
As infrastructure technologies mature, services and platform software are expected to lead in delivering added value through automation and intelligence at the network’s edge.
Fully exploiting the potential of edge intelligence requires more than enhanced IoT processing and low-latency services; it demands the development of collaborative platform environments that enable advanced services and inspire growth across the value chain.
As an Innovation Action project funded under the EU HORIZON program, COP-PILOT aims to develop a Collaborative Open Platform (COP) that enables end-to-end orchestration across service domains.
As such, COP-PILOT adds novel AI-driven tools, SLA-preserving automation, and security & trust by design into an open Pan-European platform-of-platforms, enabling diverse real-world applications across vertical sectors such as energy, smart cities, agriculture, and manufacturing to drive cross-sector collaboration, innovation, and societal impact.
